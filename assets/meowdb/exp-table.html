The EXP Formula

Four formulas represent every level from 1 to 100.

LevelsFormulaWorked example
1 to 5`(floor(level² / 2) + 15) × level`Level 5: (12 + 15) × 5 = 135
6 to 50`q = floor(level² / 3)`, then `(q + 19) × q`Level 49: (800 + 19) × 800 = 655,200
51 to 89`trunc(previous level's EXP × 1.0548)`Level 51: trunc(709,716 × 1.0548) = 748,608
90 to 99`trunc(previous level's EXP × 2.1)`Level 90: trunc(5,684,790 × 2.1) = 11,938,059
From level 51 to 89, every level costs 5.48% more than the one before it. From level 90 to 99, every level costs 2.1 times the one before it.

Party EXP

80% of the party's EXP pool is split by level and 20% by damage dealt to the monster. The party bonus is then added to each member's share.

Formula
Pool = MobEXP × PartyDamage / MobMaxHP
Share = max(1, round(Pool × (0.8 × YourLevel / EligibleLevels
+ 0.2 × YourDamage / PartyDamage)))
Bonus = min(0.30, 0.10 × (EligibleMembers - 1))
+ 0.05 × (MembersWhoDamagedIt - 1)
BonusEXP = Bonus > 0 ? max(1, round(Share × Bonus)) : 0
TotalEXP = Share + BonusEXP
  • MobEXP and MobMaxHP are the monster's base EXP and maximum HP. PartyDamage is the party's counted damage to that monster; YourDamage is your part of it.
  • EligibleLevels is the sum of eligible members' levels, including yours. EligibleMembers includes you; MembersWhoDamagedIt counts eligible members who damaged this monster.
  • Eligible members are on the same map and have attacked, taken damage, or used a skill within the previous 10 seconds.
  • Round the share first, then the bonus. Round halves up: 7.5 becomes 8. A zero bonus gives 0 bonus EXP.
  • Six eligible members with one attacker get a 30% bonus. If all six hit the same monster, the bonus is 55%. The bonus applies to your share.

Observations about the curve

  • The level 10 advancement is cheap. 3,347 total EXP to hit 10. The Maple Island questline rewards 680 EXP, enough for level 6 on quests alone, and the rest falls quickly from the mobs around you.
  • Level 50 is under 0.04% of the way to 100. 6,851,591 total to reach 50, against 18,194,778,397 to reach 100. Level 99 alone is over half the climb.
  • Almost all of the climb sits above level 90. Reaching level 90 costs 103,322,764, which is 0.6% of the total. The remaining 99.4% is levels 90 to 100.
  • The curve settles at 51, then takes off at 90. Below 50 each level costs between 1.08 and 2.76 times the one before it. From 51 to 89 the multiplier locks at 1.0548. From 90 to 99 it is 2.1, so every level costs more than double the last.

Practical grind numbers

Translating the table into on-the-ground decisions. All mob EXP values below are taken straight from the monster database and assume solo EXP.

  • Ribbon Pig (lv 10, 19 EXP). Going from 10 to 11 needs 1,716 EXP, which is about 91 kills.
  • Stirge (lv 20, 37 EXP). Going from 20 to 21 needs 20,216 EXP, which is about 547 kills.
  • Ligator (lv 31, 58 EXP). Going from 30 to 31 needs 95,700 EXP, about 1,650 kills.
  • Cold Eye (lv 40, 85 EXP). Going from 40 to 41 needs 294,216 EXP, about 3,462 kills.
  • Clang (lv 49, 127 EXP). The last level before 50 costs 655,200 EXP, about 5,160 kills solo.
  • Tauromacis (lv 70, 337 EXP). The 3rd job level costs 2,062,925 EXP, about 6,122 kills.
  • Death EXP loss. LUK lowers the EXP lost when your character dies.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to reach level 20 or 30?

In the closed online tests, testers clocked about 3 to 4 hours from level 10 to 20 and about 8 to 9 hours from 20 to 30. The first character to reach level 30 in the second closed test took 14 hours in total. Your own pace moves with class, map and party size, so measure it with the grind tracker.
